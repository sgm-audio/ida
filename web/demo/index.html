Prove the DSP, instantly.

Each panel below is a real librosa analysis of its audio, rendered by a zero-dependency widget. Press play: the live analyser measures what you hear.

These clips are synthetic stand-ins generated by python -m ida_dsp demo. Replace them with your own recordings and the same pipeline produces the panels.
